I've always been drawn to the space between what organisations do and how the world understands them — that gap is where public relations lives.

Currently pursuing my PG Diploma in Advertising and PR at IIMC New Delhi, where I ranked first in my semester, I came to communications through a background in Political Science and a deep interest in how language shapes public perception.

Over the past two years I've worked across PR agencies, OTT platforms, research institutions, and tech firms — from supporting Google's PR team on media monitoring and influencer mapping, to leading campaign strategy for Prasar Bharati's WAVES OTT, to coordinating field research at IIT Madras.

I'm actively relocating to Bengaluru, Chennai, or Hyderabad and available for full-time PR and corporate communications roles from July 2026.
